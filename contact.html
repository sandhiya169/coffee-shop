Quick answers

Quick answers.

The questions we hear most. Pick a topic, or send us a message if yours is not here.

Orders and beans

How fresh are the beans I order?

We roast in small batches and ship soon after, so your bag arrives close to its best. Every bag carries its roast date.

Can you grind the beans for my brewer?

Yes. Tell us how you brew (espresso, pour-over, French press) in your message and we will grind to match.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Message us as soon as you can with your order details. Changes are easiest before your beans are roasted.

Events and bulk

What kind of events do you work with?

Launches, team mornings, small weddings and pop-ups. Share the date, guest count and place and we will suggest a setup.

How early should I book?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for most events. Shorter notice is always worth asking about.

Do you supply cafés and offices?

Yes. Tell us your weekly volume and brew method and we will arrange samples before you commit.

Visiting us

Where can I find you?

Evolve Studio, 4th Floor, Begur - Koppa Rd, Bengaluru. The map below has directions.

When can I call?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Outside those hours, send a message and we will reply the next working day.

Can I drop in without booking?

For a quick coffee or a bag of beans, yes. For a tasting or a group visit, message us first so we can save you a table.
