BEAN TO CUP

Every cup has a long road behind it.

Five quiet steps, each one handled with the same patience, from the first harvest to the last pour.

  1. 1

    Origin

    Grown at altitude, slowly, where cool nights concentrate sweetness.

  2. 2

    Harvest

    Ripe cherries are picked by hand, then washed and dried with care.

  3. 3

    Roast

    Small batches roasted to a profile built around each bean’s character.

  4. 4

    Bloom

    Hot water meets fresh grounds, letting the aroma open.

  5. 5

    Pour

    The last pour lands in your cup, finished by hand and served singing.
